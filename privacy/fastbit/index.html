FastBit Privacy Policy

This policy applies to FastBit – Fasting & Water Tracker for Android, developed by Tech With M. FastBit works without an account.

Last updated November 11, 2026

Fasting and water records

FastBit stores fasting sessions, water intake, goals, and preferences on your device to show your history and schedule reminders. FastBit does not send these records to Tech With M or to its analytics or messaging services. Android may back up or transfer app data depending on your device, Android version, and backup settings.

Notifications and analytics

Fasting and hydration reminders are scheduled on your device. FastBit also uses Firebase Cloud Messaging for push notifications. Google processes installation identifiers, notification tokens, and topic subscriptions to deliver messages. You can control notification display in Android settings.

FastBit includes Google Analytics for Firebase. Google may collect automatic app usage events, device information, and identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID when available, to measure app usage. See Firebase privacy information and Google Analytics data collection.

Future advertising

FastBit does not currently display ads. If Google AdMob is enabled in a future version, Google may process identifiers such as the Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Deleting records and contacting us

Local records remain on your device until removed. You can clear FastBit’s local data in Android’s app settings or uninstall the app. Manage any Android backups separately through your device or Google account settings. Google handles service data under its applicable retention policies.

For privacy questions or requests to access or delete data, email techwithmonir@gmail.com. We use the information you provide to respond to your request.
