WorkQuill Privacy Policy

This policy explains how WorkQuill: Job Photo Reports, developed by Tech With M / Md Moniruzzaman, handles photos, job information and other data.

Android app: com.tech_with_monir.workquill
Effective and last updated: October 28, 2026

Your work stays on your device unless you choose to export or share it.

WorkQuill has no user account, developer cloud service, advertising or analytics. You control your photos, editable jobs, saved PDFs and portable backups. Backups are unencrypted and may contain customer details and original photo metadata.

Your data Sharing and backups Retention and deletion Contact

1. Information handled on your device

WorkQuill is an offline tool for creating job and before-and-after photo reports. It stores the information you add in private app storage on your Android device, including:

  • Job and work details: job names, customer and site details, locations you type, dates, references, notes, findings and work performed.
  • Photos and edits: photos you capture or import, captions, before-and-after selections, editable marks and working image copies.
  • Company information: the company name, contact details, logo and other profile fields you choose to enter.
  • Reports and local settings: saved PDF versions, report preferences, drafts, Trash and workspace safety copies.

This information is used locally to organize your work, prepare reports, save revisions and provide backup and recovery. Tech With M does not receive it automatically.

2. Camera and selected files

Camera permission is requested when you use photo capture. You can also select existing photos through your device's photo or gallery provider. Android's document picker lets you choose backup files to restore and destinations for exported files.

WorkQuill does not request microphone, location or contacts permission, or broad access to all files on your device. Locations and addresses that you type are part of your job content, not live device-location tracking. The app does not record audio.

3. Photo metadata and report content

Original imported photos are preserved unchanged in the workspace. They may contain embedded metadata, including capture dates, camera information or GPS coordinates. Portable backups retain those original files and their metadata.

Images prepared for PDF reports are newly encoded without source EXIF/GPS metadata. This does not remove information that you include visibly in a report, such as customer names, site addresses, dates, captions, company details or text visible in a photo. The fields, images and branding you select form part of the PDF you save or share.

4. Sharing, exports and portable backups

You choose when to share a PDF, save a copy or create a portable backup, and which destination or other app receives it. A gallery provider, document provider, email app, messaging app or cloud-storage service you select may transfer or store its copy under its own privacy policy.

WorkQuill does not operate a report-upload or backup server and does not control copies held by those services or recipients.

Portable backups are not encrypted by WorkQuill. They contain the editable workspace, including original photos, customer details, company information, saved PDFs and Trash. Store them privately. Exported PDFs are readable reports and are separate from editable-workspace backups.

The app is configured to exclude its private data from Android's automatic cloud backup and device transfer. Use WorkQuill's manual portable backup and restore tools when moving your work.

5. Advertising, analytics and automatic collection

WorkQuill has no advertising, usage analytics, advertising identifiers or built-in automatic crash-upload service. The release app has no Internet permission and does not send job content or device identifiers to Tech With M. We do not sell your personal information.

Android, Google Play and services you choose may independently handle purchase information or device diagnostics under their own policies and your device or account settings. Google Play handles the app purchase; WorkQuill does not collect your payment-card details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Current workspace: jobs, photos and saved reports remain on your device until you remove them or clear the app's storage.
  • Trash: trashed jobs remain recoverable until you permanently delete them. Trash has no automatic expiry.
  • Safety copies: previous workspace copies remain inside app storage until you remove them using Settings → Backups & storage. Permanently deleting a job from the current workspace does not remove it from an older safety copy.
  • Uninstalling: clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app's local workspace and local safety copies. Make a portable backup first if you want to keep your editable work.
  • Separate copies: gallery originals, exported PDFs, portable backups and files already shared with others remain in their own locations. Delete those separately through the relevant device, provider or recipient.

There is no WorkQuill account or developer-held job database to delete. Tech With M cannot remotely retrieve or delete work stored only on your device.

7. When you contact support

If you email techwithmonir@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use that correspondence to answer your request and handle related follow-up, and retain it as needed for that purpose. You can contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence.

The app's support note is copied or shared only when you choose that action. Photos, reports and diagnostic logs are not attached or sent automatically. Please share only the information needed for your request.

8. Data protection

WorkQuill uses Android's private app storage and performs report preparation locally. The protection of data on your device depends on Android, your screen lock, device settings and access to the device. WorkQuill does not add password encryption to its portable backups. Protection of exported or shared copies depends on the destination you select.

9. Children's privacy

WorkQuill is designed for professional job reporting and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us so we can address the request and remove that correspondence.

10. This website and external links

This privacy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to this page. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for hosting security, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and Privacy Statement.

Opening an external link or contacting support through another service is subject to that service's own privacy practices. Visiting this page does not give us access to your WorkQuill workspace.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when WorkQuill's functionality or data practices change. The effective and last-updated date above will identify the current policy. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law or platform policy.
