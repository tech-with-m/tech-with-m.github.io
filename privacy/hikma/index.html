Hikma Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Hikma: Time & Reflection for Android, developed by Tech With M. Hikma works offline and keeps your countdown profile, saved readings, and preferences on your device.

Last updated November 21, 2026

Information Hikma uses on your device

  • Countdown profile. Hikma uses your date of birth and chosen lifespan to show time lived and time remaining.
  • Lifespan reference choices. Your selected country and population reference group help Hikma suggest a lifespan from statistics bundled with the app. Hikma may read a country code from your SIM, mobile network, or device region to suggest a starting country. You can change the country in Settings → Review suggestion.
  • Reading and display preferences. Hikma saves your bookmarks, language, theme, display choices, and widget settings so you can return to them.
  • Widget diagnostics. Hikma stores information about widget refreshes, such as timing and error counts, so you can check refresh status in the app.

Storage and sharing

Hikma stores these records in Android's private settings storage. The app disables cloud backup for its data and excludes its preferences from Android device transfers. Hikma works without an account, advertising, or analytics. Hikma does not send your app data to Tech With M or share it with third parties.

Home-screen widgets

Anyone who can see your home screen can see the information on your widgets. Turn on Hide numbers on widgets in settings to keep the time figures inside Hikma. A widget can still show a progress bar.

Keeping and deleting your information

Hikma keeps your saved information on your device until you remove it. You can modify the profile and preferences or remove individual bookmarks in the app. Use Settings → Erase my data to clear your saved profile, bookmarks, and settings and return widgets to setup. Clearing Hikma's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

External links and this page

When you tap a source link in Hikma, it opens in your browser or another compatible app. The destination handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

GitHub Pages hosts this privacy policy and logs visitors' IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
