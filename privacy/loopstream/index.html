LoopStream Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the LoopStream Android application, developed by Tech With M, accesses and handles information when you publish a selected video to YouTube or Twitch and use the app's advertising-supported features. Earlier ad-free releases do not include the advertising functionality described in sections 5 and 6.

Effective and last updated: October 29, 2026

The short version

LoopStream has no developer-operated upload or relay server. Encoding happens on your Android device, and the resulting stream is sent directly to the platform you select. The current release also displays Google AdMob ads. Google processes advertising-related information as described below, with privacy choices available where applicable.

1. About LoopStream

LoopStream lets you select a compatible video using Android's file picker and publish it to YouTube or Twitch using a stream key that you provide. The app does not require a LoopStream account.

2. Information the app accesses

  • Selected video and media information: LoopStream reads the document you choose through Android's system file picker. Your chosen document provider may supply a local or cloud-backed file. The app may retain the system's read-access permission to reopen that selected document. It reads technical details such as its name, duration, dimensions, codecs, frame rate, and audio format to check compatibility and prepare the stream.
  • Stream key: You enter a YouTube or Twitch stream key so the selected platform can identify and accept your stream. LoopStream uses the key only to establish the publishing connection that you request.
  • Local app state: The app stores limited settings on your device, such as whether onboarding or platform instructions have been completed. During a publishing session, it may temporarily retain the selected platform, file name, playback mode, duration, and session timing so it can explain if Android interrupted the stream. Local information can also include the selected quality, stop timer, the latest support report, privacy choices, and the last interstitial-ad display time used to limit ad frequency.
  • Network status: LoopStream checks whether a network connection is available so it can connect, publish, and report connection problems.
  • Support and diagnostics: The app keeps a local report with app, device, Android version, media-format, encoder, and session-performance details. It excludes your stream key, video content, file name, and file URI. Selecting Copy details copies this report to the Android clipboard so you can choose whether to share it. These app-generated support reports are not automatically uploaded to Tech With M. If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and anything you choose to include. Please do not send stream keys.

3. How video and stream keys are handled

Video decoding and encoding take place locally on your device. The encoded audio and video are sent directly from your device to the YouTube or Twitch ingest service you selected. Tech With M does not receive, host, relay, or store your source video or broadcast.

Publishing connections use RTMPS, which encrypts data in transit. Your stream key is included only in the direct connection to the selected platform. When Remember this key is enabled, the key is encrypted with AES-GCM using a key protected by Android Keystore and stored in the app's private storage on your device. This option is enabled by default and can be changed on the key-entry screen. Turning it off and continuing from that screen removes the saved key for that platform. The current setup and active publishing session can still hold the key in memory while it is needed.

4. Permissions and device access

LoopStream may use the following Android capabilities:

  • Internet and network status: to publish directly to YouTube or Twitch, report connectivity, and communicate with Google's advertising and consent services.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to keep an active, user-initiated publishing session visible and provide a Stop action while the app is in the background.
  • Wake lock: to reduce the chance that Android pauses the device during an active stream.
  • Selected-file access: to read only the video you explicitly choose through the Android system picker.
  • Advertising identifiers and Android advertising services: permissions declared by the Google ads SDK support advertising delivery, measurement, attribution, and interest-based advertising. Availability and use depend on the Android version, device settings, and applicable privacy choices.

LoopStream does not request access to your camera, microphone, Android location permissions, contacts, or all files on your device. Google can still estimate a general location from an IP address, as explained in section 5.

5. Google AdMob advertising and related data

LoopStream uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial, and native ads in the app. Google's SDK collects and shares information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This can include:

  • Network information: IP address, which can indicate your approximate location.
  • Interactions: app launches, taps, and ad or video views.
  • Diagnostics: technical performance information, including launch timing, hangs, and energy use.
  • Identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other device or account identifiers where applicable.

These services can store or read identifiers and other information on your device for ad delivery, measurement, and saved privacy choices. Google's processing depends on its policies, your settings, and applicable consent choices. Tech With M can view advertising reports provided by AdMob. LoopStream does not attach your selected video, file name, file URI, or stream key to ad requests. Advertising-related analytics and diagnostics are separate from the local support report described in section 2.

6. Advertising consent and your choices

LoopStream uses Google's User Messaging Platform to check whether a privacy message or consent choice is required for your location and circumstances. Ads are requested only when that platform reports that requests are permitted. This does not mean that everyone must give consent in every region.

  • Where a consent message is presented, use its controls to review and make the available choices. You can revisit those choices through Ad privacy choices on the app's Home screen when that entry point is required.
  • Depending on your choices and Google's serving rules, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. Non-personalized ads can still use technical information and identifiers for functions such as frequency limits and aggregate reporting.
  • You can manage or reset/delete your Android advertising ID in your device's privacy or ads settings, where supported. These controls do not necessarily stop all ads or all technical data processing.

Publishing is not conditional on an ad loading or displaying. Privacy choices do not provide a universal ad-free mode. For additional Google advertising controls, see Google's advertising information and controls.

7. Streaming platforms and other third parties

When you start publishing, you direct LoopStream to transmit the stream to YouTube or Twitch. Those services may process your stream, account information, IP address, and related technical information under their own terms, channel settings, and privacy policies. Tech With M does not control their processing or retention practices.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • LoopStream does not make a developer-controlled cloud copy of your selected video. Copies or recordings retained by YouTube or Twitch are controlled through your account and the selected platform's settings.
  • A remembered stream key remains encrypted in app-private storage until you turn off Remember this key and continue from the key screen for that platform, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can also reset or revoke the key from the relevant platform dashboard.
  • Local preferences, session information, ad-frequency settings, and the latest support report are used for app operation. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app-managed local copies. A report already copied to the clipboard or shared elsewhere is outside that local cleanup.
  • Google's advertising and consent services and the selected streaming platform retain and handle information under their own policies. Clearing LoopStream's storage or uninstalling it does not itself delete data already held by those providers. Use their privacy controls or request processes for that information.
  • If you contact Tech With M for support, we use the information you provide to respond and handle the request. You may contact us to request deletion of support messages, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Because LoopStream provides no user account and Tech With M does not maintain a server-side user database for the app, there is no LoopStream account or developer-held media library to delete.

9. Security

LoopStream uses platform security features including Android Keystore, encrypted RTMPS transport, app-private storage, and Android backup settings configured to disable app backups and exclude remembered stream keys and active-session records from device transfer. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, so you should keep stream keys private and reset a key immediately if you believe it has been exposed.

10. Children's privacy

LoopStream is not directed to children under 13. Users are responsible for meeting the age, account, content, and live-streaming requirements of YouTube or Twitch and for complying with the selected platform's rules.

This policy may be updated when LoopStream's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law or platform policy.
