Kilopix Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Kilopix: Photo Compress Resize, developed by Tech With M / Md Moniruzzaman, handles your photos, image metadata, and device information.

Android Application ID: com.tech_with_m.kilopix
Effective and last updated: November 16, 2026

🔒 100% Offline & On-Device
Your photos never leave your device.

Kilopix is built from the ground up as a private, offline tool. The production application requests zero network permissions, contains no analytics or tracking code, shows no advertisements, requires no user account, and does not operate any remote servers or cloud databases. Every conversion, compression, and resize happens strictly on your Android device.

1. About Kilopix & Offline Architecture

Kilopix is an offline image utility designed to convert image formats (JPEG, PNG, WEBP, HEIC), compress file sizes (by percentage or to an exact target KB), and resize image dimensions.

The core architecture is entirely self-contained:

  • Zero Network Access: The production application does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It has no technical capability to connect to the Internet, send outbound HTTP/HTTPS requests, or upload any files.
  • No Cloud Servers: Tech With M does not host or operate any upload servers, processing APIs, or cloud storage for Kilopix.
  • No User Accounts: You do not register, log in, or provide any identity or contact details to use the application.

2. Photos and Files You Select

Kilopix only interacts with images that you explicitly select using Android's standard system pickers (the Android Photo Picker or the Storage Access Framework document picker), or when you choose to share photos into Kilopix from another app using Android's system Share sheet.

  • No Background Scanning: Kilopix does not scan your gallery, read unselected photos, or index your file system.
  • Non-Destructive Processing: Kilopix never modifies, overwrites, or deletes your original source images.
  • Saved Output: Converted and compressed photos are saved as new files in your device's standard public picture storage (under the dedicated Pictures/Kilopix album) using Android's MediaStore API so that they appear in your favorite gallery app.

3. EXIF Metadata & Location Privacy

Digital photos often contain embedded EXIF metadata, which can include the capture date, camera model, lens settings, and precise GPS location coordinates. Kilopix gives you full, transparent control over this metadata before you process any photo:

  • Strip All Metadata: Completely removes all EXIF and header information for maximum privacy.
  • Keep All Except Location: Preserves technical photo attributes (orientation, capture date, camera model) while automatically stripping sensitive GPS location coordinates.
  • Keep All Metadata: Preserves all original metadata tags.

Kilopix itself does not read, log, or track your physical location, and does not request Android location permissions.

4. Device Permissions and System Capabilities

The production release of Kilopix adheres to a strict zero-permission model:

  • No Internet Permission: The app cannot access the Internet or mobile data.
  • No Location Permission: The app does not request ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION or ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION.
  • No Broad Storage Permissions: The app does not request MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or legacy storage access. It operates within Android's modern Scoped Storage sandbox.
  • No Camera or Microphone Permission: Kilopix does not access your camera or audio hardware.
  • No Contacts or Identity Permission: Kilopix does not access your contacts, accounts, or phone identity.

5. Sharing and FileProvider

Kilopix includes an optional Share action on the results screen allowing you to send converted images directly to other applications (such as email, messaging, or cloud storage apps) via Android's system Share sheet.

When you invoke Share, Kilopix uses Android's secure FileProvider (com.tech_with_m.kilopix.fileprovider) to grant temporary, read-only content access to the specific cached output image only for the duration of that share action. Once received by another app, the copy is subject to that destination application's own privacy policy.

6. No Advertising, Analytics, or Tracking

We respect your privacy:

  • No Advertising: Kilopix contains no advertising SDKs, banner ads, interstitial ads, or sponsored content.
  • No Analytics: The app contains no third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar services).
  • No Advertising Identifiers: Kilopix does not collect or transmit the Android Advertising ID (AAID) or App Set ID.
  • No Sale of Data: Because we do not collect any data, we never sell, rent, or share personal information with data brokers or third parties.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Temporary Processing Cache: While converting or compressing photos, Kilopix temporarily stores working copies in the app's private internal cache directory (cacheDir/out/). These temporary files are cleared automatically when batch processing completes or when you clear app data.
  • Saved Output Photos: Photos you choose to save into Pictures/Kilopix are permanent files in your device's media storage. You can view, organize, or delete them at any time using your preferred Gallery or file manager app.
  • Local Preferences: User preferences (such as your chosen default format, compression preset, metadata policy, or UI theme) are stored in your device's private SharedPreferences.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application or clearing its storage through Android Settings immediately removes all internal app cache and preference data.

Because Tech With M does not operate a user account system or remote database for Kilopix, there is no online account or cloud data to delete.

8. Data Protection & Security

Kilopix relies on Android's security sandbox and platform protections. All file operations occur in app-private storage or via standard platform content resolvers. App backups to Google Drive are disabled (android:allowBackup="false") to ensure temporary working images are not uploaded during automatic device backups.

9. Children's Privacy

Kilopix does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is an offline photo utility suitable for general audiences.
