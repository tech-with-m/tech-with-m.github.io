ParaNai Vocab Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ParaNai Vocab (প্যারা নাই Vocab - IELTS Vocabulary Flashcards), developed by Tech With M / Md Moniruzzaman, handles user data, on-device storage, and audio features.

Application ID: com.tech_with_m.ielts_deshi_vocab
Effective and last updated: November 2, 2026

Your learning progress stays entirely on your device.

ParaNai Vocab is an offline-first educational flashcard app. We do not require an account, we do not collect personal contact information, and we do not transmit your study activity to any external server.

Local Data Permissions & Audio Third Parties Data Deletion Children's Privacy Contact

1. Information Handled on Your Device

ParaNai Vocab operates locally on your Android device. To power our spaced repetition system and track your learning progress, the app stores the following data in private app storage:

  • Study Progress: Records of which words you marked as “I Know This” or “Don't Know”, your current mastery stage (0 to 3), consecutive correct counts, and review timestamps.
  • User Preferences: Local configuration options such as preferred speech rate, volume, and card review modes.

All learning records are stored exclusively on your device using Android's private application storage. Tech With M does not operate a user database, and your study statistics are never automatically synced, uploaded, or viewed by us.

2. No Account or Registration Required

You do not need to register, create a profile, or log in to use ParaNai Vocab. The app does not request or collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.
  • Passwords, social media profiles, or financial information.
  • Device contacts, calendars, or stored media files.

3. Permissions & Text-to-Speech (TTS)

ParaNai Vocab adheres strictly to the principle of minimal device permissions:

  • Audio Pronunciation (Text-to-Speech): When you tap the audio icon, the app utilizes Android's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine to speak English words aloud. Speech generation takes place locally on your device. No voice or audio is recorded or stored.
  • Internet Access: Basic network capability may be declared for standard Android system checks and Google Play ecosystem compatibility. The core flashcard learning system, all 800 words, mnemonics, definitions, and IPA transcriptions are bundled with the app and function 100% offline.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, GPS/fine location, contacts, phone calls, SMS, or external storage files.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

ParaNai Vocab Version 1.0.0 does not include third-party behavioral trackers, marketing SDKs, or in-app advertising networks. We do not sell, rent, or trade user data to data brokers or third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all progress is stored solely in your device's private storage:

  • You can reset your learning progress at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > প্যারা নাই Vocab > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local flashcard progress and saved preferences from your device.
  • Because Tech With M does not retain a server-side account or database for you, there are no remote accounts or cloud records to delete.

6. Children's Privacy

ParaNai Vocab is an educational vocabulary study app designed for students and learners of English. It does not solicit or collect personal information from individuals of any age, including children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.
